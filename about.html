01 About me

About Me

I am a current 3rd-year PhD student in astronomy at UW-Madison studying exoplanets. I am also extremely passionate about science communication, teaching, and public outreach!

02 My story

Background

I was born and raised in Livonia, Michigan, where I was raised by my parents and sister. This is where I made most of my friends for life, and much of my heart still lies there.

I went to the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, but began college in engineering before stumbling my way into astrophysics (it takes time!). There, I found my footing and what I wanted to do for life.

Your trajectory will likely not be a straight line. I'm currently at the University of Wisconsin-Madison getting my PhD in exoplanet astronomy (learn more here!), but I've also really begun to branch out even more into science communication, teaching, and outreach (read more here!). Want to know some of my non-science interests? Just scroll down!
