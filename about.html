01 About me

About Me

I am an astronomy PhD candidate at UW-Madison studying exoplanets. I also work in science communication, teaching, and public outreach.

02 My story

Background

I grew up in a middle-income suburb of Detroit as the child of Indian immigrants. My parents emphasized education because they wanted me to have opportunities they had not. At the same time, I learned to navigate between their culture and that of my peers—a tension that shaped how I think about belonging and representation.

I began college in engineering before finding my way to astrophysics. The route was not obvious: I had to actively seek mentors, research opportunities, and information about what an academic career could look like. At the University of Michigan, a broader community helped me reconnect with my identity and connect service with science.

That experience now informs how I approach mentorship and community. I want early-career researchers to have the information they need to make informed choices—and to see paths into science that do not require already knowing how the system works.
