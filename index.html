02 Current research

Research areas

My research moves from the chemistry of planet-forming disks to the architecture and atmospheres of mature exoplanet systems, with the long-term goal of identifying worlds that could harbor life.

01 / Atmospheres

Exoplanet atmospheres & retrievals

Using JWST spectra, archival data, and machine learning to understand clouds and atmospheric composition on super-Earths and sub-Neptunes.

Enter project ↗
02 / Dynamics

Exoplanet dynamics

Reconstructing how planetary systems acquired their present-day orbits, including measurements of spin-orbit alignment.

Enter project ↗
03 / Origins

Protoplanetary disk chemistry

Tracing water, carbon, and organic molecules in the inner regions of disks where terrestrial planets assemble.
