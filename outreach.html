05 Teaching

Teaching & mentorship

Astronomy can invite people into science through questions that already feel personal: Where did we come from? Are there other worlds like ours? Are we alone? I want to use those questions, which drive me to do what I do, as a starting point for others to find their own interest in the Universe.

I am a graduate teaching assistant for ASTRON 104: Our Exploration of the Solar System and previously taught ASTRON 170: The Dark Side of the Universe at UW-Madison. I also worked as an undergraduate grader for statistical and thermal physics at Michigan.
