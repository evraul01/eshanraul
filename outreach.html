05 Teaching & mentorship

Astronomy as a gateway.

Astronomy can invite people into science through questions that already feel personal: Where did we come from? Are there other worlds like ours? I use that starting point to make unfamiliar ideas approachable without removing their complexity.

I served as a graduate teaching assistant for ASTRON 170: The Dark Side of the Universe at UW-Madison and as an undergraduate grader for statistical and thermal physics at Michigan. As UW-Madison’s Astronomy Telescope Lead, I also organize observing and help other students build confidence using telescopes.

Advocacy & service

Scientific opportunity should be visible, affordable, and reachable.

I participated in AAS Congressional Visit Days, served on the organizing committee for GLEAM, co-founded the Asian Service and Health Engagement Organization at Michigan, and continue to advocate for early-career researchers and more representative scientific communities.
